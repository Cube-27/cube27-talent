# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are enterprise talent-acquisition, workforce, people, and functional leaders who need to build specialist or leadership capacity without creating a separate hiring operation around every role. Candidates are a complete secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

The website generates qualified employer requirements and candidate applications for a managed talent and team-building service. It explains how we find, assess, onboard, and support talent and gives both audiences a clear route to act.

## Positioning

We combine evidence-led hiring with employee lifecycle management. Candidates complete role-appropriate recorded assessments and practitioner interviews; clients receive a curated shortlist with decision context, then continue with one partner for onboarding, administration, and ongoing support.

## Operating Context

The service supports global, remote, hybrid, onsite, and relocation-based hiring across functions and seniority. Clients choose and lead the people. Our team manages the hiring operation and ongoing support around them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One specialist, concurrent roles, leadership search, or teams built over time.
- Broad cross-functional scope; published role families are illustrative.
- AI may assist sourcing and screening, but people make every decision.
- V1 is a lead-generation website for a manual operation, not a marketplace or software platform.
- Public copy may describe benefit-level coordination of contracts, payroll, applicable local requirements, culture onboarding, and performance reviews. It excludes contractual mechanics, legal advice, pricing, guarantees, fake proof, and certification ownership.
- Existing forms, Cloudflare Pages Functions, Turnstile, Resend, upload validation, consent, and redirects remain stable.

## Brand Commitments

- Marketing prose uses first-person plural and keeps Cube27 to identity, metadata, contact, legal, and structured-data contexts.
- Primary CTA: Build your team.
- Visual direction: sharp, flat, enterprise, and vibrant; square marketing surfaces and near-square functional controls.
- Visual references: Toptal and Superhuman for craft level, not composition.

## Evidence on Hand

- 200+ positions filled — approved.
- 150+ brands served — approved and previously sourced from the parent site.
- 15+ years serving global clients — approved.
- No approved client-logo set, testimonials, case studies, ratings, or delivery-time claims.

## Product Principles

1. State the commercial outcome before explaining the process.
2. Prove hiring quality with evidence and practitioner judgment.
3. Present one accountable partner from hiring through ongoing support.
4. Keep scope broad, claims factual, and decisions human.
5. Preserve a respectful, complete candidate experience.
6. Treat compliance as an operating standard across every hire, not a recruitment vertical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All public routes must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and readable no-JavaScript fallbacks.
